Navigation

  • Drag to orbit. Right drag, Alt + drag or Shift + drag to pan.
  • Scroll to zoom toward the cursor, Shift + scroll to pan sideways. F fits the model back into view.
  • On touch: one finger orbits, two fingers pan and zoom.
  • The arrow pad at the lower left always pans, whatever the input device.

Reading the model

  • Levels isolate one floor. Exploded pulls the levels apart to show the risers.
  • The tree on the left is the hierarchy: system, subsystem, equipment. Click any row.
  • Walkthrough below plays the electrical or cooling path stage by stage, with the camera following.
  • Each equipment panel opens with one sentence on what the thing really does, then the detail.
